# Licensing and ownership review packet

This packet organizes questions for qualified counsel. It is not legal advice or a legal approval.

## Current repository position

- Licensor and copyright claimant: **Samsarix LLC**.
- Product marks claimed: **Samsarix** and **Samsarix Story Studio**.
- Public source license: Business Source License 1.1 with the parameters in `LICENSE`.
- Additional Use Grant: personal, educational, research, charitable, and internal-business production use; a paid or revenue-generating third-party substitute requires a commercial license.
- Change Date: 2030-07-28, subject to the BSL text's earlier-fourth-anniversary rule for a specific version.
- Change License: Apache License 2.0.
- Commercial and trademark contacts: `contact@samsarix.com`; support: `support@samsarix.com`.

## Documents to review together

Counsel should review `LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, `TRADEMARKS.md`, `COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `CITATION.cff`, package metadata, and the Git history as one record. Confirm that public descriptions of the Additional Use Grant match the controlling license text and that no document accidentally grants broader trademark, warranty, support, or commercial rights.

## Ownership diligence

- Identify every human, automated, bot-authored, imported, or copied contribution and the basis on which Samsarix LLC can license it.
- Confirm whether pre-LLC contributions were validly assigned to Samsarix LLC and prepare assignments if needed.
- Inspect commit authorship, prior repository names, branches, archives, generated assets, and any code moved from sibling repositories.
- Decide whether a Developer Certificate of Origin, contributor license agreement, or copyright assignment is appropriate before accepting outside contributions.
- Generate and review a dependency-license inventory for every release artifact; confirm notices and source-offer obligations where applicable.

## BSL questions

- Does the Additional Use Grant describe the intended competitive boundary clearly enough for users and enforcement?
- Is the fixed Change Date appropriate for every version, and does the earlier-fourth-anniversary clause produce the intended date?
- Is Apache-2.0 an acceptable Change License for the business model and inbound dependency mix?
- Should evaluation, consulting, managed internal use, embedding, resale, and API access be addressed more explicitly?
- Should a separate commercial license template define fees, support, updates, warranties, indemnity, data handling, termination, audit, and governing law?

## Brand and product questions

- Conduct trademark clearance for Samsarix and Samsarix Story Studio in intended jurisdictions and classes.
- Decide whether and when to register the word marks and any logo.
- Confirm that the fork/compatibility policy is enforceable and does not overreach nominative or other lawful use.
- Review provider names in documentation for proper nominative use and required notices.

## Approval record

Before relying on exclusivity or issuing a commercial license, record counsel, jurisdiction, review date, exact Git commit, approved documents, required edits, unresolved risks, and the authorized Samsarix LLC signer. A repository maintainer should then implement the approved text in a dedicated reviewed commit.
